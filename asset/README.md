# Shared asset plan — Home by Christmas

Status: shared production inventory remains planned. A limited three-asset raster style trial is saved in `trial/`: Tommy's hands/sleeves, the M1 Garand, and Annie's letter/star. No animation or audio has been generated. The supplied screenshot is preserved in `reference/soldier-style-reference.png`.

All future asset files belong under `asset/`. Use the existing BG / HD / SP / PR / FX / TX / AU IDs. The proposed subfolders below are destinations, not completed assets.

## Selection rule

Choose assets that appear in at least two scenes, or reusable components that connect otherwise different scenes. Priorities below are recommendations based on continuity, screen time, and story importance. The separate outdoor component kit is a proposed reuse strategy, not an additional scripted background.

Source: [production script](/Users/sengzhe/Project/makathon/doc/Home%20by%20Christmas%20%E2%80%94%202.5D%20Production%20Script.md). Scene numbers below refer to that script. This is a shared-asset shortlist, not the complete production inventory.

## Recommended generation order, when generation is authorized

1. Lock Tommy's hands/sleeves/rifle/pocket and the letter/star masters.
2. Lock the medic and boy, including the close views required in S5.
3. Build the kitchen and Field C as separate layers, with the foxhole separated.
4. Expand the riflemen/body libraries, continuity props and shared terrain.
5. Add drawn FX elements and compositor masks; apply scene grades afterwards.

This prioritization is a recommendation for the complete production masters. Only the three selected static style trials in `trial/` have been generated.

## Avoid these traps

- Do not flatten a whole character, held prop and background into one image. Keep the script's L7a–L7d hand/rifle/prop separation. [Hand construction](/Users/sengzhe/Project/makathon/doc/Home%20by%20Christmas%20%E2%80%94%202.5D%20Production%20Script.md:139).
- Do not bake the opening halftone, battle desaturation or flare orange into the only master. The script changes grades across scenes. [Colour arc](/Users/sengzhe/Project/makathon/doc/Home%20by%20Christmas%20%E2%80%94%202.5D%20Production%20Script.md:44).
- Do not treat the British/German reference costumes as Tommy's costume. His jacket and rifle are specified separately in the script. [Tommy's costume and rifle](/Users/sengzhe/Project/makathon/doc/Home%20by%20Christmas%20%E2%80%94%202.5D%20Production%20Script.md:141).
- Shared design does not mean identical perspective: the medic's close-up, letter in different hands, and the ground-level foxhole need matching view-specific art.

## Scope notes before future generation

- BG-00 is plain black; plan it as a compositor fill, not generated artwork. [Background list](/Users/sengzhe/Project/makathon/doc/Home%20by%20Christmas%20%E2%80%94%202.5D%20Production%20Script.md:327).
- The newspaper, grenade, Karl, machine-gunner and wall soldier remain necessary later even though their main appearances are confined to one scene. This shortlist does not replace them. [Full master list](/Users/sengzhe/Project/makathon/doc/Home%20by%20Christmas%20%E2%80%94%202.5D%20Production%20Script.md:319).
- SP-10 is listed for S3–S5 in the master inventory; S4 specifies its body counts most clearly. Keep the library planned across those scenes, but confirm placement during layout. [Body list](/Users/sengzhe/Project/makathon/doc/Home%20by%20Christmas%20%E2%80%94%202.5D%20Production%20Script.md:350).
- The HD library includes the injured HD-10i variant. The first-person hands share components across S1–S5, but the rifle is picked up in S3; it should not appear in Tommy's hands during breakfast. [Pickup](/Users/sengzhe/Project/makathon/doc/Home%20by%20Christmas%20%E2%80%94%202.5D%20Production%20Script.md:160).
- The script specifies 1920 × 1080 playback, 2× painted backgrounds and panorama widths of 3200/2600 px. Proposed convention: interpret those panorama widths as logical widths and export kitchen at 6400 × 2160 and field panoramas at 5200 × 2160, before extra overscan. Resolve this interpretation and rotation margins before generating backgrounds. [Canvas specification](/Users/sengzhe/Project/makathon/doc/Home%20by%20Christmas%20%E2%80%94%202.5D%20Production%20Script.md:73).
